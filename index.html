Ministry of Sports and Tourism · Official Fan Platform

One nation.
One giant screen.

The Government of Uganda, through the Ministry of Sports and Tourism, is staging the biggest public viewing celebration in the country's history for the FIFA World Cup 2026. Nine verified fan zones — from Kololo Airstrip in Kampala to Buhinga in Fort Portal — bring every match live, loud and free to Ugandan fans. 01 Uganda at World Cup 2026 · Your Official Fan Pass

Kampala joins the world's biggest football party

For six weeks, Ugandan fans will follow every kick of the tournament together. The Ministry, working with host city councils and the National Council of Sports, has equipped nine official fan zones with giant LED screens, floodlights, medical posts and security screening. Match nights feature live broadcasts, half-time entertainment from local artists, and appearances by Cranes legends — all free of charge for registered fans.

To keep every venue safe, entry runs on a digital fan pass. Registration is free and takes about five minutes:

  1. Check you are eligible. Fans aged 12 and above may register; younger children enter with a registered adult.
  2. Gather your identification. Have your National ID (NIN) or passport details ready before you begin.
  3. Create your fan profile. Dial *284# or visit any Ministry registration desk at the nearest city council offices.
  4. Choose your fan zone and match dates. Select one home zone (for example, Kololo Airstrip) and the sessions you wish to attend.
  5. Receive your QR fan pass. Your pass arrives by SMS and email within minutes — save it or print it.
  6. Show pass plus matching ID at the gate. Security scans your code; wristbands are issued for re-entry on the same day.
02 Gate Guide · What You Can Bring

Pack the passion, leave the trouble

Every official fan zone is screened at the gate for everyone's safety. The following items are welcome inside all nine venues:

  • National flags and cloth banners without poles longer than one metre
  • Small bags no larger than A4 size, subject to a quick search
  • Sealed non-alcoholic drinks in plastic bottles up to 500 ml
  • Vuvuzelas, foam sticks and drums for designated cheering corners
  • Personal phones and small cameras for photos with friends
  • Rain jackets and ponchos — June evenings can be wet
  • Empty reusable water bottles for the free refill stations
  • Baby strollers and changing bags in the family viewing areas

Prohibited items — weapons, glass bottles, fireworks, drones and professional camera lenses — are displayed at every gate and on this portal. Confiscated items are not returned.

Official Gazette · Ref MOTS/WC26/041 · Published 14 August 2026

Ministry confirms all nine verified fan-zone locations — warns against fake ticket vendors

The Ministry of Sports and Tourism wishes to inform the general public that only the nine venues listed on this portal are licensed to operate as official FIFA World Cup 2026 fan zones. Following recent confusion caused by fake ticket vendors and unofficial "viewing centres", the Ministry has completed physical inspection of every site, including screen capacity, emergency exits, sanitation and crowd-safety certification. Kololo Airstrip in Kampala remains the national main stage with a combined screen view for 15,000 fans, supported by regional hubs in Jinja, Mbale, Gulu, Mbarara, Fort Portal, Entebbe, Wakiso and Masaka.

Entry to all official fan zones is free and strictly by registered digital fan pass. No agent, promoter or website is authorised to sell tickets for these venues, and no payment should be made to anyone claiming otherwise. Fans are urged to verify every location on this portal before travelling, and to report imposters through the Ministry's toll-free line 0800 202 026 or the police hotline 999. Organisers reserve the right to close any unlicensed gathering advertising itself as an official fan zone.

— Permanent Secretary, Ministry of Sports and Tourism
