The programme below lists confirmed tournament moments and fan-zone events. Match pairings are verified against official FIFA broadcast listings each morning, and any changes are announced on the home page before 10:00 EAT.

01 Tournament Programme · June–July 2026
Thu 11 Jun · 22:00 Opening Ceremony & Opening Match — live from Mexico City Gates 17:00

Screened live at all nine fan zones. National ceremony at Kololo Airstrip from 19:00 with performances by top Ugandan artists.

Fri 12 Jun – Sat 27 Jun Group Stage Triple Headers — daily marathon screenings From 18:00 daily

Kololo screens all three daily matches; regional zones join from the second match. Half-time DJ sets in every cheering corner.

Sun 21 Jun · 16:00 Cranes Day — celebrating Ugandan football culture All venues

Legends' exhibition replay, traditional dance troupes and the biggest vuvuzela orchestra attempt at Lugogo Cricket Oval.

Sun 28 Jun – Tue 7 Jul Knockout Nights — Round of 32 & Round of 16 Evening sessions

Late-night transport arranged with city councils. Fan passes required for each knockout session — register early on *284#.

Fri 9 Jul – Sat 11 Jul Quarter-Final Double Bills From 20:00

Extended family areas open at Mbale, Mbarara and Gulu for the quarter-finals, with children's football clinics before kick-off.

Tue 14 & Wed 15 Jul · 21:00 Semi-Final Big-Screen Nights Gates 17:00

Both semi-finals screened back-to-back at every venue. Predict-and-win competitions run on the big screen between matches.

Sat 18 Jul · 18:00 Fan Appreciation Night — Kololo Airstrip Free concert

Pre-final music concert headlined by Uganda's biggest acts. Wristbands from the 17 July sessions grant priority entry.

Sun 19 Jul · 22:00 The Final — live from New York / New Jersey Stadium Gates 18:00

The closing celebration of the tournament. Trophy replica photo booths at all nine zones from 19:00.

Kick-off times are converted to East Africa Time and may change if FIFA reschedules fixtures for weather or logistics. This portal is the only source of verified times — beware of social-media pages quoting different schedules.
